```text
**Welcome to the UOR Foundation**

We're an independent nonprofit (501(c)(3)) building open tools so any piece of data can prove what it is, wherever it goes.

**Why it matters**
Today, data is found by *where* it lives: a link, a file path, a database key. Move it or change one byte, and that label breaks or quietly points at something else. You end up trusting whoever hosts the data, not the data itself.

**How UOR solves it**
UOR (Universal Object Reference) gives every piece of data an address worked out from its own content. Same content, same address, on any machine. Anyone holding the data can check it themselves. No central registry, no permission needed. The specification is open and mathematically checked.

**Learn the UOR Framework**
The framework explainer covers why it exists, how it works and what you can build, written for anyone with no prior knowledge of UOR.
https://universal-coordinate-foundation.lovable.app/framework/
Specification updates and open discussion live in #uor-framework.

**Prism SDK**
Prism is our open SDK. It lets you build, ship and run self-verifying AI apps, from edge to cloud.
Prism SDK v1.0 is in development. Scope, progress and what ships next are on the public roadmap:
https://github.com/orgs/UOR-Foundation/projects/12

**Find your way around**

**First steps**

Glad you're here.
```
